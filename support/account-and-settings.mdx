The sidebar on the left holds every page. The organization switcher sits at the top of it, and Support and your profile icon sit at the bottom.

## The sidebar

**Analytics**

| Page | What it shows | Tabs |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [Citations](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/citations) | The domains and pages AI answers cite | Most Cited Domains, Most Cited Pages, My Pages |
| [Fact Checker](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/fact-checker) | Pages on your site with conflicting or outdated information (Enterprise and Managed Solution plans) | |

| [Ads Analyzer](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/ads) | Ads ChatGPT shows next to answers for your prompts (plans with Ads Analyzer) | Overview, Advertisers, Prompt Insights, Ad Creatives |

| [Reddit Analyzer](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/reddit-listener) | Reddit threads that mention your brand or keywords (plans with Reddit Analyzer) | Reddit Mentions, Keyword Tracking, Settings |

Pages that your plan does not include show an upgrade prompt.

You can also ask **Ask Pierview**, at the top of the sidebar, any of the questions below.

## Your account

**Log out.** Click your profile icon at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Sign out**.

**Change your login email, name or password.** Click your profile icon, then **Manage account**. To change your email, add the new address, verify it, and set it as primary.

**Change plan or billing.** Owners and admins: profile icon, **Manage account**, then **Subscription Plan** or **Manage Billing**.

## Organizations and workspaces

**Switch organization or add one.** Click the organization name at the top of the sidebar. Pick another organization, or choose **Add organization**.

**Delete an organization.** Owners and admins: profile icon, **Manage account**, **Subscription Plan**, then **Delete** beside the organization. The organization keeps full access until the start of your next billing cycle, and is deleted then. Its prompts, responses, citations and reports are no longer available after that.

**Invite or remove a teammate.** On [Team](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/team), enter their email, pick a role (Admin, Member or Viewer) and whether the invite covers the whole workspace or one organization, then send the invite or copy the invite link. **Remove** is beside each member. Viewers cannot invite.

## What you track

**Add, edit, duplicate or remove a persona.** Go to **Prompts**, then the [User Personas](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/prompts/personas) tab. **Add Persona** creates one, and each persona has **Duplicate**, **Edit** and **Remove**. To track the same audience in another country, duplicate the persona and change its location in **Edit**.

**Change the location you target.** For the whole organization, go to **Settings**, the [Organization](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration) tab, and change **Geography**. For a single audience, edit that persona's location on **User Personas**.

**Add, edit or delete prompts.** On [Prompts](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/prompts), use **Add prompt**. Deleted prompts are kept on the **Deleted Prompts** tab.

**Add or remove competitors.** Go to **Settings**, then the [Competitors](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration/competitors) tab, and use **Add competitor**. Give a competitor aliases so other spellings of its name are matched.

## Connections

**Connect Google Search Console or Google Analytics.** Go to **Settings**, then the [Data Sources](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration/data-sources) tab. See [Google Search Console and Analytics](/integrations/google-search-console-and-analytics).

**Install the tracking script.** Copy it from the **Traffic Analysis** tab of [Traffic Analysis](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/referrals) and add it to every page of your site.

**Choose which events count as leads.** **Traffic Analysis**, the [Lead Tracking](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/referrals/lead-tracking) tab, then **Configure lead tracking**.

**Connect a CMS or create an API key.** **Settings**, then the [CMS Integrations](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration/integrations) or [API Keys](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration/api-keys) tab.

**Use Pierview from Claude or ChatGPT.** See [MCP server](/integrations/mcp-server).

## Content

**Write a new article.** Go to [Content Creation](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/content-creation) and click **Create new content**. In the Create Content table, type a topic under **Article Topic (Input)** (use **Add Row** for more, or **Generate Suggestions** for topics drawn from your AI visibility gaps), pick the **Content Type**, then click **Start Workflow**. Confirm the title and the outline when asked, and the article is written. Publish it with **Publish Article** once a CMS is connected under **Settings**, [CMS Integrations](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration/integrations). To write and publish on a schedule, use **Auto-Publish** on the Content Creation page.

**Improve a page you already have.** Use [Optimize Content](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/optimize-content): import the page, review what to change, and rewrite it.

## Reports and exports

**Download a report.** Click **Report** at the top right of any dashboard page, then choose a report type. For an **Actionable Report** (recommended actions drawn from across the platform), click **New Report**, set the **Timeframe** and click **Generate Report**. It takes about 5 minutes and is saved in the list on the left: select it, then **Download PDF** or **Download HTML**. For a **Data Report** (the underlying data, such as AI SEO audits, sentiment and citations), tick the **Report Sections**, set the **Date Range**, then **Download PDF** or **Download HTML**. Set the report logo under **Settings**, [Reports](https://www.pierview.ai/dashboard/configuration/reports).

**Export as CSV.** **Export CSV** is on Prompts (prompts with mention and citation rates), Citations, Ads Analyzer (ad sightings) and Lead Tracking. The visibility chart has no CSV export. Set it to daily to read one value per day, or ask **Ask Pierview** to list daily visibility as a table.

## Get help

Click **Support** at the bottom of the sidebar. Describe the problem and choose **Submit Request**, or use **Email Support** or **Schedule Call**.
